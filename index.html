Premisave

Payments and wallet services for businesses in Kenya and beyond.

Premisave provides a wallet and a payment service for businesses. Businesses that want to accept payments apply for a merchant account, upload their registration documents, and are reviewed by Premisave staff before they are approved.

Google Drive access

The Premisave service uses Google Drive only to store the documents that applicants upload with a merchant application, in a single folder owned by the Premisave operator. See the privacy policy for details.
