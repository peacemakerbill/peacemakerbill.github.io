Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

This policy explains what the Premisave service does with the information it handles, including its use of Google Drive.

What we collect

When a business applies for a merchant account, we receive the details it enters (business name, registration number, tax number, address, contact details, directors) and the documents it uploads, such as a business registration certificate, a tax compliance certificate, a director identification page and, where someone applies on a business's behalf, an authorisation letter.

How we use Google Drive

The Premisave service connects to one Google account, the account of the Premisave operator, to store the uploaded documents in a single Drive folder. It reads, writes and deletes files in that folder only so that Premisave staff can review applications. It does not read any other files in that Drive, and it does not access any other person's Google account or data.

Premisave's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who can see the documents

Documents are visible to the applicant and to the Premisave staff who review applications. We do not sell them, use them for advertising, or share them with third parties, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep them

We keep application documents for as long as they are needed to review the application and to meet our legal and compliance duties. An applicant can ask us to delete a document that is no longer needed by writing to the address below.

Security

Access to the Drive folder is limited to the Premisave service and the account owner. Access credentials are kept on the server and are not shared.

Contact

For questions or requests about this policy, write to thepeacemakerske@gmail.com.
